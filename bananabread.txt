Ingredients:
- 150 g flour
- 50 g sugar
- 100 g olive olive
- 3 ripe bananas
- 2 eggs
- 2 tsp baking powder
- 1 tsp cinnamon, ground
- 1 pinch sea salt

Preparation:
Preheat the oven to 180°C and line a cake tin with baking paper
Bruise the bananas, then add to a bowl and mash with a fork
Add sugar and cinnamon, stir
Add eggs, stir
Sift flour and baking powder over the bowl (to prevent clumps), stir
Add olive oil and sea salt, stir
Pour into the cake tin, heat for 45 min
