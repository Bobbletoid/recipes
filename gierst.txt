Ingredients (2p):
- 100 g millet
- 100 mL coconut milk, unsweetened
- 150 mL poultry stock (or chicken)
- 2 tsp raisins
- 4 dried apricots, chopped
- 1 pinch turmeric, ground 
- 1 pinch cinnamon, ground 
- 1 slice fresh ginger
- 2tbsp almonds, chopped
- 1tbsp shaved coconut
- 1 sprig fresh parsley, chopped

Preparation:
Wash millet under cold water and drip dry
Add millet, coconut milk, stock, raisins, apricots, turmeric, cinnamon and ginger
    to a pan and gently boil
Lower the heat and let it simmer until almost dry - never fully dry (usually 15-20 min)
Remove the ginger and stir in the almonds and coconut shavings
Garnish with parsley
