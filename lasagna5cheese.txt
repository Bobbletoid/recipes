1) Ingredients, red sauce:
- 500g minced meat (optional)
- 1 zucchini, diced
- 1 eggplant, diced
- 200g carrots, diced
- 1 onion, chopped
- 800g tomato sauce/frito 
- 200g tomato puree (3 cans)
- 2 cloves garlic, chopped
- 1tsp Italian herbs
- pepper and salt, to taste
- 50g butter + extra for oven dish
- 2tbsp olive oil

2) Ingredients, white sauce:
- 2 or 3 eggs
- 2tbsp fresh parsley
- 400g cottage cheese
- 100g ricotta
- 60g parmesan cheese

3) Ingredients, layers:
- lasagna sheets (varies)
- 90g provolone (or emmentaler), grated
- 225g mozzarella, grated

Preparation, sauce:
Add butter and olive oil to a big, heavy-bottomed pan and heat
Add all ingredients for red sauce (1))
Simmer for 1 hour without lid, stir periodically

Preparation, topping:
Add eggs to a large bowl and gently stir
Add all other ingredients for white sauce (2)) and gently stir

Preparation, lasagna:
    (OPTIONAL: refrigerate the dish one day before heating to make it more firm)
Preheat oven to 190°C. Grease a big oven dish with butter
Add a thin layer of red sauce (1/4), then cover with lasagna sheets
Add provolone/emmentaler (2/3), then a thin layer of white sauce (1/3)
Add mozzarella (1/3), then cover with lasagna sheets
Add a layer of red sauce (2/4) and provolone/emmentaler (1/3), then cover with lasagna sheets
Add white sauce (2/3) and mozzarella (1/3), then cover with lasagna sheets
Add red sauce (1/4) and mozzarella (1/3)
Cover dish with aluminium foil, heat for 50 min (+20 min if refrigerated)
Remove aluminium foil, heat for 20 min (optional: add grilling so it colours the top nicely)
Leave dish to rest for 15 min before serving
