Ingredients:
- 45 g butter
- 165 g almond flour
- 2 ripe bananas
- 3 eggs
- 1tsp vanilla extract
- 1 tsp cinnamon, ground
- 1 tbsp baking powder
OPTIONAL
- 30 g walnuts, unroasted, chopped
- 30 g pure chocolate flakes
- 1 small bananas, cut lengthwise

Preparation:
Preheat oven to 175°C and line a cake tin (20 cm) with baking paper
Melt better and cool down
Bruise bananas, then add to a bowl and mash with a fork
Add eggs, vanilla extract and butter, beat with a mixer until smooth
Sift almond flour and baking powder over another bowl and add cinnamon, stir
Add the second bowl to the first and beat with the mixer
OPTIONAL: Add most of walnuts and chocolate flakes
Pour into the cake tin
OPTIONAL: Gently ress the banana halves, inside up, into the batter and add rest of walnuts
Heat for 45-50 min or until done (stab it with a knife, see if it comes out dry)
    Check periodically, add aluminium foil if top gets too brown
Let it rest/cooldown enough to not burn your mouth before serving
